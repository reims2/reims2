# Reports

In **Reports**, download a `csv` for the current location: the current inventory, everything dispensed or
deleted, or searches without matches. Needs internet.

## What to refill

**What to refill** is experimental. It lists five kinds of glasses to look for when filling the
free SKUs, and how many of each. It looks for past patients that no pair in today's stock would fit,
and picks the ranges where the pairs this site has had most often fit one of them. The top row helps
the most patients. Each row asks for about as many pairs as the site has had in that range, since
more would mostly repeat prescriptions. Fill the remaining SKUs with any glasses. The list updates as
stock is added or dispensed.

Patients who still find nothing get glasses made at the site, so the list aims to help as many
patients as possible, not every one.
