# Dispense and undo

Dispense right on a result in [Find](./find), or by SKU:

1. In **Edit**, type the SKU.
2. Click **Dispense**.

![Edit page](/screenshots/dispense.png)

::: tip Keyboard
Press Enter to dispense.
:::

## Wrong pair?

Click **Undo** on the result, or under **Recently dispensed or deleted** in Edit. This works until the SKU
is given to a new pair.
