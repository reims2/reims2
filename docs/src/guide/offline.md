# Work offline

Find, dispense, edit and the inventory work without internet. Add, Reports, changing the location and this
guide don't.

This needs Google Chrome, and REIMS2 opened online on that device in the past month. Log in on every device
before the campaign, a login lasts about 7 weeks.

## Sync status

The bottom of the menu shows the sync status. Click it while online to refresh.

![Offline banner and sync status](/screenshots/offline.png)

- **Synced**: online and up to date.
- **Offline**: no connection. A banner in the bottom right says so too.
- **N waiting**: changes made offline. They upload by themselves once you're back online. On phones, a
  chip in the header shows them.
- **Outdated**: the inventory is older than 3 days. Go online once to refresh it.

Changes from other devices only show up once you're online again, so a pair may already be gone. Go online
at least once a week if you dispense or edit offline.
