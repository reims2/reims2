# Find glasses

1. In **Find**, pick single or multifocal and fill in both eyes.
2. Click **Search glasses**. Best match first, lower score is closer.
3. Try the pair on, then click **Dispense**.

![Find page](/screenshots/find.png)

::: tip Keyboard
Type `s` or `m` for the category and press `S` to search.
:::

## Results with a flask

A flask next to the score marks a suggestion from a new, experimental matching method. It predicts how
sharp the patient will see with the pair. At most every third result is one. Try it on like any other.

## No matches?

Check the input, then turn on **Wider tolerance**. It accepts lenses up to 1.0 off in sphere and cylinder
instead of 0.5. Still nothing: tick **BAL lens** for the eye that sees worse. Only the other eye is matched
then, and the BAL side just needs a lens of similar strength so both look alike.
